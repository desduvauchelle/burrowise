# Burrowise Design System

## 1. Direction

Burrowise is a subterranean field desk: a calm, capable workspace for following ideas deeper without losing the trail. The interface borrows from field notebooks, soil, moss, and contour maps—not literal rabbit illustrations. It should feel grounded, private, and exploratory.

## 2. Color

- Warm stone is the application ground; paper is the primary reading surface.
- Forest green is the structural and interactive color.
- Clay marks capture and other high-intent actions.
- Moss communicates positive state; ochre communicates caution.
- Dark mode uses charcoal-green rather than blue-black.

## 3. Typography

Use Avenir Next where available, followed by SF Pro Text and system sans. Headlines are compact and confident with tight tracking. Labels use sentence case; uppercase is reserved for genuinely terse metadata.

## 4. Layout

The persistent navigation is a dark forest anchor. Content uses generous warm negative space, while lists and controls remain dense enough for daily operation. The existing three-pane information architecture is preserved.

## 5. Shape

Controls use 7–10px radii and major containers use 12–14px. Avoid excessive pills. Borders are quiet and warm; shadows are reserved for floating or elevated elements, not every card.

## 6. Interaction

Hover states shift tone and position subtly. Focus rings are high-contrast and visible. Motion stays under 200ms and respects reduced-motion preferences.

## 7. Iconography

Use the existing consistent outline icon family. The Burrowise spiral mark represents a path descending into a burrow. Icons support labels rather than replace them in ambiguous actions.

## 8. Accessibility

Maintain WCAG AA contrast for body text and controls, 38px minimum desktop control targets, visible keyboard focus, and equivalent meaning across light and dark themes.
